Roles & Positions

Alamin ang estratehikong tungkulin ng bawat manlalaro mula Position 1 hanggang Position 5 sa Dota 2.

Strategic Purpose of Each Position

In Dota 2, each team role from Position 1 to 5 has a distinct strategic purpose. Position 1 (Carry) focuses on farming early to become the late-game damage dealer who secures victory in team fights. Position 2 (Mid) controls tempo, often rotating to other lanes to pressure enemies and create space. Position 3 (Offlane) disrupts the enemy carry, builds durable items, and initiates fights to protect their own team. Position 4 (Soft Support) roams, provides vision, and sets up plays with disables or utility. Finally, Position 5 (Hard Support) secures the lane for the carry, buys wards, and sacrifices farm to ensure the team’s overall success.

Position 1

Safe Lane Carry

Nangunguna sa gold at items para maging pangunahing tagapanalo sa late game team fights.

Position 2

Mid Laner

Nangunguna sa levels, nagdidikta ng bilis ng laro, at nagro-rotate para mag-gank sa ibang lanes.

Position 3

Offlaner (Initiator)

Makunat na frontline tank na unang lumulusob at gumugulo sa carry ng kabilang koponan.

Position 4

Soft Support (Roamer)

Aktibong nagro-roam, nagse-set up ng kills, at bumubuo ng utility items para sa buong team.

Position 5

Hard Support

Protektor ng carry sa safe lane, bumibili ng wards, at nagbibigay ng map vision.
